# Kubernetes

- [Documentation](https://kubernetes.io/docs/home/)

## Notes

> Your containers manage your apps. K8s manages your containers.

And your storage, keys, logs, resources, DNS, etc. Consider is a generic platform for managing "deployments" of containers and integrations for stuff surrounding it.

### Terminology

- A `Pod` can be thought of as a running container.

<div style="max-width: 90%; text-align: center; color: var(--emphasis-color)">
  <PodsImg />
</div>

- You application may consist of many pods.

<div style="max-width: 90%; text-align: center; color: var(--emphasis-color)">
  <AppsImg />
</div>

- A `Node` is analogous to a "machine" or "server" (physical/virtual) that would normally host your apps. A node has many pods.

<div style="max-width: 90%; text-align: center; color: var(--emphasis-color)">
  <NodesImg />
</div>

- A `Control Plane` is a node that controls the other "worker" nodes, and is a core part of every k8s deploy.

<div style="max-width: 90%; text-align: center; color: var(--emphasis-color)">
  <ControlsImg />
</div>

- A `Cluster` is a collection of nodes and the control plane.

<div style="max-width: 90%; text-align: center; color: var(--emphasis-color)">
  <ClustersImg />
</div>
